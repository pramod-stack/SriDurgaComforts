Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you arrive.

Clear, honest answers regarding our location on NH-75, room amenities, elevator access, and booking procedures.

Have More Questions? Call Us
Where is Sri Durga Comforts located? +
Sri Durga Comforts is located on the Mangalore–Bangalore Highway (NH-75) at BHM Arcade in Javaranahalli, Bellur, Nagamangala Taluk, Mandya District, Karnataka 571418. It is conveniently situated near Adichunchanagiri University.
Is Sri Durga Comforts directly accessible from NH-75? +
Yes. The property has direct frontage on NH-75 at Bellur Cross. You can pull in seamlessly from the highway without navigating through village by-lanes or unpaved detours.
How close is the hotel to Adichunchanagiri University and Math? +
Sri Durga Comforts is situated adjacent to the Adichunchanagiri University campus and within easy driving distance to the historic Adichunchanagiri Math pilgrimage shrine. For exact navigation routes, tap "Get Directions" or view our location on Google Maps.
What rooms and facilities are available? +
We offer comfortable Double Rooms in both Air-Conditioned (A/C) and Non-A/C choices. Each room comes equipped with an attached western bathroom, dedicated hot water geyser, wall-mounted television, full wardrobe, and dresser with mirror.
Is there an elevator / lift in the building? +
Yes, a modern passenger elevator operates in the building, making check-in to our 2nd-floor reception and guest rooms effortless for seniors, families with luggage, and all guests.
How can I book a room and check current tariffs? +
You can contact owner Raghavendra and our reception team directly by calling +91 99757 28106 or +91 73491 64171, or by submitting the online enquiry form below which opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message. We confirm tariffs and availability directly to ensure complete accuracy.
What are the check-in and check-out timings? +
Please inform us of your expected arrival time when you call or message on WhatsApp. We provide flexible assistance based on daily occupancy and room preparation.
